Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Inkwell Monkey for Mac, by Amaki Studio

Inkwell Monkey does not collect, upload or track any data. Your comics and your reading stay on your Mac.

What the app does with your files

  • Inkwell Monkey only reads the comic files and folders you choose to add. It opens them read-only: it never moves, copies, renames, changes or deletes them.
  • Nothing from your comics, not even a file name, is sent anywhere. All reading happens on your Mac.

What is stored on your Mac

To remember where you are, the app keeps a small library record inside its own sandboxed folder on your Mac: the folders you added, reading progress, series groups, chosen covers and your settings. It also keeps cover thumbnails and temporary page caches there. A short record of which books are free in the free version is kept in your Mac's keychain. None of this leaves your Mac. You can remove it at any time with Settings › Library › Clear Library…, or by deleting the app.

If you choose Settings › Library › Export to Downloads, the app writes a backup file of your reading progress and groups to your Downloads folder. It goes nowhere else.

AI features run on your Mac

AI Enhance (sharpening old scans) and Smart Grouping (recognizing the same series in different languages) run entirely on your Mac using on-device models. No images or titles are uploaded.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

No analytics, ads or tracking

Inkwell Monkey has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't know who you are or what you read.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions about privacy: hello.amakistudio@gmail.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日 · 墨水猴（Mac 版），Amaki Studio 出品

墨水猴不收集、不上传、不追踪任何数据。你的漫画和阅读记录只留在你自己的 Mac 上。

App 怎样对待你的文件

  • 墨水猴只读取你自己添加的漫画文件和文件夹，而且只读：从不移动、复制、改名、修改或删除它们。
  • 漫画里的任何内容，包括文件名，都不会发到任何地方。所有阅读都在你的 Mac 上完成。

Mac 上保存了什么

为了记住你读到哪里，App 在它自己的沙盒文件夹里保存一小份书库记录：你添加的文件夹、阅读进度、系列分组、自定义封面和你的设置。封面缩略图和临时的页面缓存也存在那里。免费版哪几本书可以读，有一小份记录存在 Mac 的钥匙串里。这些都不会离开你的 Mac。你随时可以在 偏好设置 › 书库 ›「清空书库…」里清掉，或者直接删除 App。

如果你点 偏好设置 › 书库 ›「导出到「下载」」，App 会把阅读进度和分组的备份文件存到你的「下载」文件夹，不会存到别处。

AI 功能在你的 Mac 上运行

AI 增强（把老扫描放大到屏幕清晰度）和智能整理（认出同一部作品的不同语言版本）全部用本机模型在你的 Mac 上运行，不上传任何图片或书名。

购买

免费试用和一次买断都由苹果通过 App Store 处理。我们看不到你的姓名、Apple 账户或付款信息。苹果如何处理购买，见苹果隐私政策。

没有统计、广告和追踪

墨水猴没有使用统计、没有广告、没有追踪、没有账号，也没有自己的服务器。我们不知道你是谁，也不知道你在读什么。

儿童

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果这份政策有变化，新版本会发布在本页，并写明新的生效日期。
