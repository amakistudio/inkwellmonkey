Inkwell Monkey — Sample Comics for App Review
==============================================

These files are for testing Inkwell Monkey (Mac App Store). Everything here
is in the public domain, CC0, CC BY 4.0, or made by Amaki Studio. Inkwell
Monkey itself contains no comics and never downloads any; it only reads
files the user already has, in place, without changing them.

How to use
----------
1. Unzip this file.
2. In Inkwell Monkey, click "Add Comics" and choose the folder
   "Inkwell Monkey Review Comics" (or drag the folder onto the window).
3. 14 books appear (shown as 9 shelf items: series are grouped). All of them are readable in the free version
   (the free version allows the first 20 books added).

What each item shows
--------------------
- Pepper&Carrot/ (3 episodes, CBZ) .... grouped into one series; click it to
                                        expand; double-page reading (press 2)
- Hokusai Manga/ (2 volumes, CBZ) ...... right-to-left reading, two-page spreads
- Little Nemo/ ......................... one PDF and one plain image folder
                                        (two formats, one series)
- One More Chapter (Webtoon).cbz ....... vertical scrolling (webtoon mode)
- The Lamp on Harbor Street.epub ....... text book reader (horizontal)
- 霧の港（縦書き）.epub .................. text book reader (Japanese vertical)
- Atomic Mouse, Blue Bolt, Jetta,
  Skyman (CBZ, cover only) ............. single books on the shelf
